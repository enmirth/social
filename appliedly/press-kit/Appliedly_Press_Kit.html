2. Approved Press Boilerplates

Short Description (25 Words)

Appliedly is a calm job application tracker with a curated recommendation inbox, tailored cover letters, market briefings, and automatic exclusions for companies already in your pipeline.

Standard Description (65 Words)

Modern job seekers are caught between fragile spreadsheets and noisy auto-apply spam. Appliedly restores calm and precision to the job hunt. Combining a structured visual pipeline with time-in-stage tracking, tailored cover letter drafts, market briefings, and a curated recommendation inbox governed by an Exclusion Contract, Appliedly ensures candidates never waste time on duplicate roles or broken tracking sheets.

3. Core Product Pillars

Calm Pipeline

Track every role across clear deterministic stages (Saved, Applied, Interviewing, Offer, Archive) with duration metrics and private notes.

Exclusion Contract

Your active Applications list acts as an automatic shield—Appliedly never recommends companies you have already applied to.

Market Briefings

Receive high-signal role recommendations, market briefings, and tailored cover letter guidance aligned to your target positions.
