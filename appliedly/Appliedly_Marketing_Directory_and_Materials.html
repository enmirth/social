1. Strategic Positioning & Core Value Proposition

Appliedly solves application fatigue and search chaos for intentional modern job seekers. While legacy platforms overwhelm users with noisy notifications and spreadsheets quickly degrade into unmanageable clutter, Appliedly provides a serene, structured application pipeline paired with high-precision AI recommendations.

The 3 Unfair Advantages to Market:
  • Calm Pipeline Tracker: Clear status stages (Saved, Applied, Interviewing, Offer, Archive) with duration tracking and notes.
  • The Exclusion Contract: AI recommendations automatically filter out companies already in your active pipeline. Zero duplicates. Zero spam.
  • Tailored Market Intelligence: Curated recommendation inbox, market briefings, and bespoke cover letter guidance.
Primary Target Audiences:
  • Tech & Knowledge Workers: Developers, designers, and PMs tracking 20–100+ intentional applications.
  • Career Pivoters: Candidates targeting new industries who require tailored positioning and market briefings.
  • Spreadsheet Exhausted Applicants: Users struggling with brittle Notion templates and Excel formulas.
